# AI Policy

This project values human creativity, engineering judgment, and thoughtful contributions.

AI-assisted development is welcome. However, AI is a tool, not a contributor, and its use does not diminish the responsibility of the human submitting the work.

## Authorship and Ownership

Every contribution must have a human who understands, owns, and takes full responsibility for it.

- AI tools must not be credited as authors or co-authors, including through `Co-authored-by` commit trailers.
- Contributors must understand and be able to explain the code, design decisions, and trade-offs behind their changes.
- Contributors are responsible for the correctness, security, quality, and maintainability of their work.
- AI assistance does not transfer or diminish responsibility.

**AI may assist in producing code, but accountability always belongs to the human contributor.**

## Contribution Quality

AI-assisted contributions are held to the same engineering standards as any other contribution.

We expect contributors to:

- Review and understand all generated changes before submitting them.
- Validate behavior through appropriate tests and verification.
- Keep changes focused, intentional, and relevant.
- Respect existing architecture, conventions, and project requirements.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity, speculative changes, and unrelated modifications.

Passing tests or compiling successfully does not, by itself, establish correctness.

We reserve the right to reject contributions that lack sufficient understanding, verification, or technical justification, regardless of how they were produced.

## Communication

Issues, pull requests, and discussions should reflect the contributor's own understanding and judgment.

AI tools may assist with writing, translation, or clarification, but contributors must personally review and take ownership of their communications.

Do not submit fabricated explanations, unverified claims, or generated responses that you cannot independently substantiate.

## Automated Contributions

AI agents may assist with planning, implementation, testing, and code review.

However, fully autonomous, unreviewed contributions are not acceptable.

A human must remain accountable for every submitted change and be available to discuss, revise, and maintain it.

## Maintainer Discretion

Maintainers retain the right to request changes or reject contributions that do not meet the project's standards.

The use of AI is neither a justification for lower quality nor, by itself, a reason for rejection.

---

**AI assistance is welcome. Human ownership is required. Engineering responsibility cannot be delegated.**
